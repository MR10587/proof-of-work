Role card 2: Backend and platform engineer
What it is. Designs services, data models, APIs and the internal platforms other engineers use.

Demand signals. Postings mention API design, databases, queues, scaling, ownership of services, on-call.

Core stack. One typed language (Go, Java, C#, TypeScript or Python), Postgres, Redis, a queue, Docker, a cloud.

90 days.

Days 1 to 14: 01 CLI expense tracker (L1).
Days 15 to 40: 03 REST API with auth (L2).
Days 41 to 70: 06 CI/CD and observability pipeline (L3), applied to your API.
Days 71 to 90: 09 Audit and harden (L4).
